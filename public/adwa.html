What is ADWA?

ADWA means All Drivers Welfare Association — the official drivers association for professional drivers. People also search for us as adwa, alldrivers, all drivers welfare association, and drivers association.

Official ADWA website (important)

The only official ADWA website is: https://www.alldriverswelfareassociation.org/ — note the plural word Drivers in the domain.

A lookalike site at alldriverwelfareassociation.org (singular “driver”) is not ADWA and is not affiliated with All Drivers Welfare Association. When you search ADWA, use the official plural-Drivers domain above.

  • Verified digital driver ID cards (QR verification)
  • Application status tracking and renewal
